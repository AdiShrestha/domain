Aditya Shrestha

B.E. in Computer Engineering · Kathmandu University, Nepal
AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate

I am an undergraduate researcher focusing on self-supervised representation learning for real-world time-series and sensor data. My primary interests include environmental early-warning systems, real-time ML pipeline infrastructure, and topological anomaly detection in transaction networks.

My work is built around pre-registered falsification protocols, transparent reporting of negative results, and lightweight, reproducible software engineering.
