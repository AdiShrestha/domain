Background & Focus

I am a final-year Computer Engineering undergraduate at Kathmandu University in Dhulikhel, Nepal. My research focus centers on self-supervised representation learning for sensing and temporal series, environmental early-warning systems, and low-latency machine learning streaming systems.

I prioritize methodology and negative-result integrity in student research. For example, my work on glacial lake outburst flood detection implements a pre-registered falsification protocol with transparent reporting of both success margins and failure envelopes.
